# AquaLens — USP & Positioning

## One-liner
**AquaLens is the only citizen-science tool that interviews you like an ecologist —
catching your observation errors *while* you make them, and showing its work.**

## The 3 differentiators (judge-facing)
1. **Validation at collection time, not after.** Everyone else cleans data post-hoc with
   expert review. AquaLens cross-checks answers mid-interview, when the citizen can
   still look at the water again.
2. **Explainable by design, human always in charge.** Every flag says *why* in plain
   language; the citizen keeps or changes each answer. No black box, no overridden
   judgment — exactly what Track 3 asks for.
3. **From observation to One Health story.** The output isn't a spreadsheet row — it's a
   health snapshot with a plain-language story linking stream signals to ecosystem and
   human wellbeing, readable by residents, researchers, and policymakers alike.

## 30-second pitch
"Urban streams are monitored by citizens, but citizen data is noisy — and today's tools
only catch errors weeks later through expert review. AquaLens flips that: a conversational
companion guides a 60-second stream check, cross-checks your answers for ecological
contradictions on the spot — 'crystal-clear water with a rotten smell doesn't add up,
want to recheck?' — and produces an explainable health snapshot with a One Health story.
The human always has the final say. Cleaner data in, better decisions out."

## Honest framing (say this before a judge asks)
"AquaLens doesn't pretend to be machine learning. It's an explainable, rule-based
assessment companion encoding real field-ecology heuristics — which makes it auditable,
offline-capable, and honest about its limits. That's the responsible-AI position."
